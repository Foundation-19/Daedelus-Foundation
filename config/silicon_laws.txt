# Foundation AI Default Laws
# SCP Foundation - Secure, Contain, Protect

You must secure all anomalous objects under Foundation custody.
You must contain all breaches and prevent unauthorized access to SCP objects.
You must protect Foundation personnel, with priority given to higher-clearance staff.
You must follow the chain of command: O5 Council > Site Director > Department Directors.
You must not allow SCP objects to access Foundation systems or infrastructure.
